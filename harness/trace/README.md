# Satoshi's crop: the trace, and what it was judged on

Every image here is regenerated by a script beside it, so anything that
looks stale can be rebuilt rather than trusted. Run them from the repo
root with `DYLD_FALLBACK_LIBRARY_PATH=":/opt/homebrew/lib"` in front,
which is what `render.sh` does for cairo.

The traced fringe **is** in the shipped cut, from the owner's crop. Five
readings off the full drawing failed first; they are written up in
`fringe.py` and `fringe2.py`. An earlier claim here that the fringe could
not be drawn came from one of those failures and is retracted.

## Satoko's hair, traced and shipped

| Image | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `satoko_grid.png` | her chibi reference with a pixel ruler, for calibration |
| `satoko_check.png` | the trace on the reference: magenta the mass, cyan the hairline |
| `satoko_port.png` | the traced silhouette against the one she used to wear |
| `satoko_shipped.png` | canon, chibi and adult as they now render |

`satoko.py` reads **the drawn black outline**, not the crop's alpha, which is a
rough selection. That works on a crop of hair alone because it has no brows and
no eyes in it, so the furthest ink along a bearing is the mass and the nearest
is the hairline. Below the cheek the hairline is scanned by rows instead, since
a fall runs too near vertical for a radial sweep to describe.
