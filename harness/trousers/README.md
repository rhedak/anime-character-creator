# The trouser rebuild: what was measured, and what it was judged on

Every image here is regenerated by a script beside it. Run them from the
repo root with `DYLD_FALLBACK_LIBRARY_PATH=":/opt/homebrew/lib"` in
front, which is what `render.sh` does for cairo.

| Script | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `measure.py` | the measurements, printed. Both references and both of our renders, through the same code |
| `see.py` → `see.png` | the lower body, canon against ours, both builds, matched on the belt-to-floor span |
| `loud.py` → `loud.png` | the garment on palettes well outside the one it was tuned on |
| `scale.py` | face width off the sheets, an attempt at a calibration that was not needed in the end |

A `hipfall.py` sat here too, comparing five rates at which the trousers
could leave the hip. It went with the hip flare it was choosing between:
the owner's call was that a leg tapering from the hip reads as thick, and
the two outer edges now run straight, so there is nothing left to pick.

## Why the measurements are taken off the silhouette

Picking the trousers out by their own colour was tried first and fails on
both sheets. The canon shades the cloth with folds and the chibi is a
JPEG, so a tolerance wide enough to hold the whole leg also holds the
boots, and one tight enough to exclude the boots breaks a single row into
a dozen runs. The background is flat near-white on both, so "not
background" is exact, and it answers the question that mattered most
directly: background between the legs is a gap, and no background is an
inseam drawn on solid cloth.

Two things still had to be kept out of the silhouette. Skin, because a
chibi's arms hang at very nearly the hip's own width, so no x-window
separates a hand from the body and the widest run in a row below the belt
is otherwise a hand. And then the hand's black *outline*, which survives
the skin test, which is why a run only counts as leg when it overlaps the
middle 60% of the belt's width.

## What it said

- The tunic is tucked: its green ends 23px above the belt's lower edge on
  both sheets.
- The legs part about a quarter of the way from the belt to the floor,
  0.28 chibi and 0.23 adult, and the slot opens to about a third of the
  garment's width by the boot.
- The garment swells 8% from the belt to its widest point.
- Our chibi's leg is 0.24 of its belt's half-width against the canon's
  0.39. That one is recorded in `docs/gap-analysis.md` and was left
  alone: it is `leg_half_w`, not anything the trousers can fix.
